Paige Marie Oamek is a writer, editor, and researcher in Brooklyn (by way of Iowa).

Paige is a founder and editor of the New York Review of Finance. She's also a markets reporter at Sherwood News.

Before that Paige was a breaking news reporter at Gothamist/WNYC and research editor at WIRED.

Paige writes about money, politics, privatization, and city life with an eye toward technology’s role and is available for freelance projects. Their writing has appeared in The Nation,The New Republic,Teen Vogue, and elsewhere.

She also writes a newsletter about good ledes.
